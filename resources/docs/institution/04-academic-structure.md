## Structure

Faculties > departments > programmes > courses. Courses can be imported from CSV (`code, title, credit_units, level, department_code` — see the **Courses** template); rows with an existing code are updated.

## Semesters and course offerings

A **course offering** is a course running in a semester, with its lecturers, capacity and streams. On the **Offerings** tab:

- tick several courses to act on them together: set status, set capacity, add or remove a lecturer, or **copy content from the last time the course ran**;
- *Streams* splits a large class into groups.

## Closing a semester and starting the next

On the **Calendar** tab: close a semester (only once every course result is released — its courses are archived and registration closes), make the next one current, and carry courses over from the same semester last session, with their content if you want.

**Automatic rollover**: switch it on in Policy. Every night after a semester's end date, the platform closes it once results are released, starts the semester whose dates have begun and carries last session's courses over. Administrators and registry are told what happened, or why it is waiting.
