## Requests from students

Students ask from *Requests & exceptions* (missed exam, deadline, registration, accessibility, hardware, result query), with evidence if they have it. Evidence is encrypted and opened only by the people who decide; every opening is logged.

Administration > Exceptions: approve or decline **with a reason** — the student sees it. Nobody decides their own request.

## Hardware exceptions

For a candidate who cannot use a webcam, microphone, second camera or a single screen (disability, no suitable device): record the exception, choose exactly which checks are switched off, and for which exam or until which date. **A second person must approve it** before it applies. The candidate's exam then simply does not ask for those checks; everyone else is unchanged.
