Each course has weekly sections with pages, files, assignments, quizzes, forums and live-class links. Items can be released on a date or after a previous item is completed.

## Materials students can read on any phone

Add materials to a week with **Add**. Students read them inside the course, with nothing to install:

- **PDF** pages are drawn in the page (with zoom); **Word**, **PowerPoint** and **Excel** files are shown as a readable page, slide by slide or as a table, with their pictures and equations.
- **Pictures and diagrams** open full screen with a tap. In a *Page*, use **Insert a picture or diagram** to put one in the text.
- **Video**: upload an MP4, or paste a YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive link; it plays in the page. **Audio** files play too.
- Students can **mark an item done** (a video counts when watched to the end, a PDF when its last page is reached), **save** it, keep **private notes**, **download** the original and **ask about it**. Previous and Next buttons go through the course in order.

## Reviews, polls and requests

The course page has tabs for:

- **Assessments**: every assignment and quiz with its due date; students see what is to do, submitted or marked (with the score), lecturers see how many handed in and how many wait for marking.
- **Polls**: ask the class a quick question. Choose whether students see the results after voting, when the poll closes, or never.
- **Requests**: students ask for a topic, material or more explanation the course has not covered; classmates add their vote. Answer with *Planned*, *Covered* (and link the material) or *Answered*; everyone who asked is told.
- **Reviews**: students rate the course (1–5 stars) with a comment, with or without their name. Reply to a review, or hide one that is offensive or off-topic.

## Assignments and marking

- Submissions are timestamped with a receipt; late work gets the penalty you set.
- **Similarity**: every submission is compared with the class and earlier classes on your own server. A high score is a prompt to read, not a verdict.
- **Outside plagiarism service**: if your institution has connected one (Integrations), a *Check with …* link appears next to each submission, or every submission is checked automatically. The score and report link appear next to the built-in one.

## Attendance

Course > Attendance. Add a class, mark it, or show a short code students type to mark themselves. Card readers and attendance apps can send attendance directly (Integrations). Attendance can count towards exam eligibility (Policy > minimum attendance).

## Recorded lectures

On the attendance page, choose a class and attach its recording: a link to your video service, or upload the video/audio file (large files upload in pieces and resume after a dropped connection). **Publish** it when ready; students see it under *Recorded lectures* on the course page.
