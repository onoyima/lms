Each course has weekly sections with pages, files, assignments, quizzes, forums and live-class links. Items can be released on a date or after a previous item is completed.

## Assignments and marking

- Submissions are timestamped with a receipt; late work gets the penalty you set.
- **Similarity**: every submission is compared with the class and earlier classes on your own server. A high score is a prompt to read, not a verdict.
- **Outside plagiarism service**: if your institution has connected one (Integrations), a *Check with …* link appears next to each submission, or every submission is checked automatically. The score and report link appear next to the built-in one.

## Attendance

Course > Attendance. Add a class, mark it, or show a short code students type to mark themselves. Card readers and attendance apps can send attendance directly (Integrations). Attendance can count towards exam eligibility (Policy > minimum attendance).

## Recorded lectures

On the attendance page, choose a class and attach its recording: a link to your video service, or upload the video/audio file (large files upload in pieces and resume after a dropped connection). **Publish** it when ready; students see it under *Recorded lectures* on the course page.
