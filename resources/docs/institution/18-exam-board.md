**Exam board** in the menu.

## Who sees which exams

- The exams office (anyone with *Run exams*) sees every exam.
- Any other member of staff sees only the exams assigned to them: named as an examiner on the exam (Exams › an exam › People), or teaching the exam's course in the exam's semester.

On their exams, examiners can monitor the sittings, add students, upload questions while the exam is a draft, see the statistics and grade spread, and download and rescale the scores. They cannot approve, schedule or publish.

## The board

**Running now** lists the exams with a sitting in progress and how many students are writing. Open an exam for:

- the counts: candidates, writing now, submitted, not started, awaiting marking;
- each sitting, with **Monitor** to open the camera wall;
- **Statistics**: average, median, highest, lowest, spread, passed and below the pass mark;
- **Grade spread**: students in bands 0–10, 11–20 … 91–100 % of the maximum;
- the **score board**: every student, their status, start and submit times, time taken, score, percentage and the score out of the scale you chose. Scores stay empty while a student is still writing. The page updates itself while anyone is writing.

## Rescaling

A paper of 240 questions marked out of 240 can be shown out of 100, 70, 60 or any number: type it under **Scores out of** and press **Show**. **Download scores** gives a spreadsheet (CSV, opens in Excel) with the scaled column. **Use … as the official total** makes it the exam's total so results and transcripts use it; this is possible until results are approved.

## Uploading questions with symbols

Under **Questions** (or **Import questions** in the question bank) choose **Science & maths**. It reads Word files directly and keeps:

- equations built with Word's **Insert › Equation** (fractions, powers, roots, sums, integrals, brackets, matrices, vectors);
- Greek letters and signs from **Insert › Symbol** or typed in the Symbol font (α, β, π, Ω, ±, ≤, →, ∞ …);
- superscripts and subscripts typed with Word's x² and x₂ buttons (H₂SO₄, Fe³⁺, 10⁻⁶, x²);
- tables, pictures (PNG/JPG), bold and italics, and Word's own 1., 2. and A., B. numbering.

You see every question exactly as students will before anything is saved. Old equation objects (Equation 3.0 and MathType) are read too: fractions, roots, powers and subscripts, sums and integrals, brackets, matrices, accents and chemistry arrows become maths like the newer Word equations. If one cannot be read (a damaged object), the preview marks it so you can click it in Word, choose **Convert**, and upload again. Text files saved by Notepad in any encoding keep their symbols.

## Monitoring

The camera wall shows every student writing, with their name and matric number on their picture, a coloured dot for their state (writing, paused or locked, offline, submitted), seat, warnings and time left. A red outline means the student needs attention (help request, locked, or several warnings). Click a picture for the student's timeline, snapshots, recordings and actions. Scores are never shown here.

Recordings run for the whole time a student is writing. If the connection drops or the student reopens the exam, recording carries on as a new part; parts are listed in order on the student's timeline.

## Exam network

**Exam network** in the menu (exams office).

- **Server address** lists the addresses this computer has on the network. If students reach it on a different address (for example 10.10.0.109 instead of 192.168.0.12), type that one under **Address students should use**; the platform then answers for your institution on it. The page shows the address and a QR code to put on the hall screen.
- **Hall computers**: for exams with *Only computers approved by IP address* switched on (Exams › an exam › Rules), a computer appears here the first time a student presses Start on it. **Approve** it (or **Approve all waiting**) and the student presses Start again. **Refuse** blocks it.
- Approvals are cleared 15 minutes after each sitting ends (you can switch this off), or at once with **Reset approvals**. Cleared lists are never deleted: **History** shows every list and which computers were allowed or refused.

## Exam rules worth knowing

- **Students who arrive late**: use the sitting's late-entry time, allow starting at any time while the sitting is open (even 30 minutes or more in), or stop new starts after a number of minutes.
- Students with attempts left see **Re-attempt** in amber instead of Start.
- A student can be signed in on one computer at a time. Signing in elsewhere signs the first computer out; an exam open there stops and carries on, with every answer, when opened on the new computer.
- **Research** is an exam type for projects and theses, scored like a viva: each examiner picks a score from a list running from 0 to the highest mark you set. Viva scores use the same list.
