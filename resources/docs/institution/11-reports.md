Administration > Reports. Each report opens on screen and exports to **Excel, CSV or PDF**. You see only the reports, and the faculties or departments, your role allows.

- Exports with personal data above the size set in Policy need a second person to approve the export request.
- Course gradebooks export from each course; question banks from the question bank; exam results from the exam.
- Your reporting warehouse can receive course-level figures every night (Integrations > Analytics feed) without personal data.
