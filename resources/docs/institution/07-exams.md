## Exam categories

A **category** groups related exams: a *Nursing exam*, a *Semester exam*, a *Departmental exam*, *GST*… **New exam category** (menu, dashboard or Exams page): name the category, then add its exams — **Objective**, **Theory**, **Objective and theory**, **Practical**, **Viva**, **Project defence** — as many as you need, more than one of a type too. Each exam can be an exam, test, quiz, mock or practice, and can have its own course.

Every exam in a category is separate: its own questions, its own students, its own examiners, its own time and its own proctoring (on or off, as set in its rules). The category page shows each exam with its next step (questions, date, students, examiners, rules, approval).

**Students** can be added
- to the **whole category**: they sit every exam in it, including exams added later (an exam without a date gets them as soon as it has one); or
- to **chosen exams only** (the **Students** button on an exam).
They come from a course's class list, a programme and level, or a pasted list of matric numbers or e-mails.

**Results**: each exam's card has PDF, Excel and CSV downloads once marking has started. **Combined results** gives one row per student with each exam's score, the total and the percentage (ABS = assigned but did not sit; Pending = not fully marked), in PDF, Excel or CSV.

## Setting a paper

Exams > New exam. Formats: objective (CBT), essay, mixed, practical (criteria with allowed score steps, several examiners, average) and viva (one overall criterion). Each section can draw *N* questions at random from a larger set. If your institution switches on paper approval (Settings › Policy), the paper goes to a second person before it can be published; otherwise **Publish** is one click.

## Examiners for one viva, practical or defence

For viva, practical and research (defence) exams, **Examiners** > *Examiner accounts for this exam only*: enter the examiner's first name, last name, a username and a password, then give those to the examiner (often an external examiner).

- They sign in with the username and password and see only this exam's scoring sheet, plus the meeting room if the viva is held online. Every other page sends them back to the sheet.
- One computer at a time: a second sign-in is refused while the first is active. If they move computers or their browser closed, press **Reset session**; they can then sign in again anywhere. Signing out also frees the account.
- When the exam is over (its last sitting has ended, or the exam is closed or archived) the account stops working, including any page left open.
- **New password** sets another password; **Remove** stops the account at once. Scores already given stay.

## Rules and proctoring

On the **Rules & proctoring** tab choose which checks apply: tab and focus checks, full screen, webcam snapshots, face presence, microphone and **noise alerts**, screen sharing, a **second camera** (phone or USB), live video, recordings, identity checks, and whether the desktop app should **close screen-sharing and remote-control apps**. Allowed channels: browser, desktop exam app, Safe Exam Browser.

**Warnings (strikes).** Leaving the exam screen — another tab, another window or app, or out of full screen — is one warning per departure. The candidate sees *Warning 2 of 5* and why. At the limit (5 unless you change it) the exam is **locked**, *submitted*, or only *reported*, as you choose; a locked exam continues only when an administrator or invigilator unlocks it from Proctoring. Answers are kept throughout.

**Before the exam starts.** Tick the checks a candidate must pass on screen before **Start exam** works: browser, internet connection, computer (not a phone), power, screen, full screen, keyboard, camera, face, microphone, sound, screen sharing, secure exam app, room scan. **Select all**, pick a few, or **None**. A room scan is a 20-second video of the room and desk taken right after starting, before the paper opens, and kept as evidence. Choosing *Secure exam app* means the exam can only be taken in the desktop exam app or Safe Exam Browser.

**Connection and power cuts.** *Time given back for a computer that went off* (30 minutes by default, for the whole exam) and *Wait for a disconnected computer after time is up* (120 minutes). See *Running exam day*.

## Checks before the exam

Under **Before the exam starts**, choose all, some or none of the checks. With none, candidates start straight away; only what the exam itself uses (the camera, if it is on; full screen, if required) can stop it starting.

## Emergency exams

Switch on **Emergency exam** on the exam's page for an exam that must run at once. While it is open, its students see only that exam when they sign in, press **Start exam**, and the questions appear. There are no checks, no monitoring notice, no invigilator check-in, and no camera, microphone, screen or approved-computer rules. Time it in the room; the on-screen timer still runs and the exam still closes at the end of its time. A sitting PIN, if you set one, is still asked for. When they finish, students have their usual pages again.

## Safe Exam Browser

**Exam day > Safe Exam Browser.** Upload the Windows and macOS installers so candidates download SEB from your own server (it works on a campus network without internet); without them, candidates are sent to the official download page. Set the **school SEB password**: every exam's SEB settings file is encrypted with it, SEB asks for it before the exam opens, and it also locks SEB's own settings on that computer. Candidates then sign in with their own account. An exam can have its own SEB password (Rules & proctoring > Safe Exam Browser).

While SEB is open nothing else appears over the exam: on Windows it runs on its own locked desktop, so other programs' notifications (including Phone Link) do not show; on Mac and iPad it uses Apple's assessment mode, which silences notifications. In an ordinary browser tab no website can hide the computer's notifications — for exams where that matters, allow only the desktop exam app and Safe Exam Browser. The desktop exam app keeps its window above everything and turns system notifications off during the exam, then back on.

## Sittings and candidates

Add a sitting (hall or remote), then candidates: from course registration, from a parent exam (resits), or by pasting matriculation numbers (see the **Exam candidates** template). The timetable generator avoids clashes and respects each student's daily limit.

## Integrity review

After the exam, the **Integrity review** tab lists attempts whose pattern is statistically unusual for that exam — scored far above what the time taken predicts, hard questions answered correctly very fast, many identical wrong answers shared with one other candidate, most answers saved at the last minute, or a high proctoring score. Clear a flag, or open an incident so the full procedure applies. Marks are never changed here.

## Setting up a paper in four steps

The top of every exam page shows what is left: **Questions**, **Students and time**, **Rules**, **Publish**. Each step opens the right tab.

- **Questions**: on the Paper tab, *Upload a file* puts the questions straight on the section; *From banks* puts on chosen questions or whole banks; a **random-draw rule** gives each student their own set — from one bank or several, with *at least N from each bank* so every topic is covered. If a rule asks for more questions than its banks hold, the page says which bank is short and by how many, and the paper cannot be published until it is fixed.
- **Publish**: one click. Papers need a second person's approval only if your institution switches that on (Settings › Policy).
- **Students**: on a sitting, *Upload a list* (CSV, Excel or text, with the old system's columns firstname, middlename, lastname, email, matric_no, password — students without an account get one after a preview), *Find a student*, *Paste matric numbers*, or *By matric / e-mail* (everyone whose matric number starts with, or whose e-mail is at, what you type). A student who has not started can be taken off again.
- **Staff promotion exams**: choose the kind *Staff promotion exam*; its candidates are staff, added the same ways, and they find it under *My exams* in their menu.

## When a student reports a question

During the exam a student can press **Report this question** and say what is wrong. It costs them no time. The exam's **Question reports** tab gathers the reports by question; for each you can correct the answer, give everyone full marks, take the question out, or keep it. Every student who had the question is marked again.

## Exam centres without internet

Under **Exams › Exam centres**, add each centre; it gets its own key. On a sitting, *Package for the centre* downloads a signed .cbtpkg file (paper, pictures, candidates, times). The centre, running its own copy of the platform, adds the main server with the same code and key, reads the package, and runs the sitting; candidates sign in with their usual password. Afterwards the centre downloads *Scripts for the main server* and you add them on the sitting with *Add the centre's scripts*: each script is stored once and marked with your answer keys. A changed file, or one signed with another key, is refused.
