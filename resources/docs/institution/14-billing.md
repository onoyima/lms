Institution settings > Subscription & billing (institution administrators only).

- Your plan, what it costs, the student allowance and when it renews.
- **Invoices** arrive two weeks before each renewal. Pay by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack (*Pay now*), or by direct bank transfer quoting the invoice number — the platform team marks it paid when it arrives.
- If an invoice is unpaid 14 days after it is due, access is suspended; your administrators can still sign in to pay, and paying restores access immediately. Nothing is deleted.
- To change plan or add a module (for example live proctoring), contact the platform team.
