**Live classes & meetings** in the menu, or **Live classes** on a course page.

Live classes run in one of two places:

- **The built-in room** works straight away, with no meeting server: each person's browser sends their camera and microphone directly to the others, and the platform only introduces them. It suits small meetings of up to 6 people, such as supervision, office hours, a viva with its panel, or a project defence. There is no recording.
- **The meeting server (BigBlueButton)** is for full classes, recordings, breakout rooms and the whiteboard. It needs a server, added by the platform team or by your institution.

On the meeting form, **Where the meeting runs** is *Automatic* by default: the meeting server is used when one is connected, and the built-in room otherwise. You can also pick one yourself. Everything around the meeting happens here: scheduling, who may come in, attendance and recordings. The meeting room itself opens in a new screen with your institution's colours.

## Scheduling

1. **Schedule**, then choose the kind of meeting (live class, office hours, tutorial, seminar, viva, department meeting and so on).
2. Choose the **course**. Everyone registered for it can join. Lecturers can schedule for their own courses; meetings without a course need the *Host meetings* permission.
3. Set the start, length and time zone. Tick **Repeats every week** and pick the days to create a whole term's classes at once.
4. **Room rules**: waiting room, microphones off on entry, recording allowed or automatic, what students may share, and who may watch recordings.

## In the room

**Start the meeting** (hosts can open the room 15 minutes early). Students can join once a host has started it, unless *Students may join before the host* is ticked. With the waiting room on, a host admits each student from the room.

In the room: camera, microphone, screen sharing, chat, raise hand, reactions, polls, whiteboard, shared notes, breakout rooms, presentations (PDF and Office files) and recording. Host tools: mute everyone, lock settings, make someone presenter, end the meeting for everyone.

Roles: the person who scheduled it is **host**. Give others **Co-host** (they run the room), **Presenter**, **Examiner** or **Proctor** under *People and roles*. Lecturers of the course are always hosts.

## Room set-up

Under **In the room** on the meeting form:

- **Screen layout**: smart (adjusts to what is shared), slides first, cameras first, or custom.
- **Students join listening only** for large lectures, and **Skip the echo test**.
- **Switch off in this room**: breakout rooms, polls, shared notes, chat, screen sharing, external videos, captions, virtual backgrounds and more.
- **Viva, practical or defence held here**: link the exam. Its examiners become hosts, its candidates may join, and the meeting page lists the candidates with a **Score** button that opens the scoring sheet on that candidate.

**Slides and documents for the room** (on the meeting page, before or during the class): upload up to five PDF, PowerPoint, Word or image files. The first opens on screen when the room starts.

## The built-in room

**Join** opens the room in the browser; allow the camera and microphone when asked. At the bottom are the controls: microphone, camera, share screen, raise hand, chat, people and leave. Hosts also see **Mute everyone**, **Mute** and **Remove** for each person, the people waiting to come in (**Let in** / **Refuse**) and **End for everyone**. The waiting room, microphones off on entry, and the camera, microphone and chat locks all apply here too. Attendance is recorded the same way as on the meeting server.

If someone cannot connect, they may be on a network that blocks direct connections, such as some office or campus networks. Moving to mobile data usually helps; for a permanent fix, the platform team can add a relay (TURN) server.

## Guest links (people without an account)

On the meeting page, **Make a guest link** creates a link for an external examiner, a guest speaker or a parent; add a passcode if you like. Guests type their name (and, optionally, their e-mail), wait until a host lets them in, and see only this meeting. Their name is marked *(guest)* and they appear in attendance. **New link** stops the old link working; **Switch off** closes it completely.

## Attendance

Attendance is recorded from the room itself: each time a person joins and leaves. Reconnections are joined together, a person in two tabs is counted once, and the meeting ending closes everyone's time. **Present** means at least half the scheduled time; joining more than 10 minutes late shows as **late**. Download the list as Excel, PDF or CSV. Students see only their own attendance.

## Recordings

Recordings appear after the meeting once the server has processed them (this can take as long as the meeting itself). Use **Look for new recordings** if one is missing. Hosts choose who may watch (hosts, invited people, or the whole institution), can hide or delete a recording, and every viewing is recorded in the audit trail. The recording's address is never shown: each viewing uses a personal link that lasts 30 minutes.

## Transcripts and translation

When the platform has a speech-to-text service set up, hosts see **Make a transcript** beside each published recording. The transcript is made in the background and can be downloaded as captions (VTT, SRT) or plain text by anyone who may watch the recording. With a translation service set up too, **Translate** makes a copy in French, Arabic, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo and other languages, with the same timings.

## Reports

**Reports** on the Live classes page: choose a course (and dates) to see each class (who came, turnout, late, partial, average stay) and each student across all classes, with those who never attended or stayed less than half the time counted at the top. Download as Excel, PDF or CSV.
