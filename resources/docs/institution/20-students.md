- **My courses**: notes, assignments, quizzes, forums and recorded lectures. Submit work before the deadline; you get a receipt.
- **Course registration**: choose your courses while registration is open; clashes and unit limits are checked for you.
- **My exams**: your timetable and exam slips. Open an exam a few minutes early to check your computer, camera and connection. The computer check also tells you if your browser is not supported.
- **During an exam** your answers save as you type; if the power or network fails, come back and continue with the time you had left.
- **My results**: published results, statements and transcripts as PDFs.
- **Requests & exceptions**: ask for a missed-exam arrangement, deadline change, accessibility arrangement or exam hardware exception, with evidence. You will see the decision and the reason.

## Live classes

If your institution uses live classes, your lecturers' classes appear under **Live classes & meetings** (and **Live classes** on each course page). Open the class a few minutes early, press **Test** to check your camera and microphone, then **Join**. If the lecturer uses a waiting room, wait until you are let in. Your attendance is recorded from when you join and leave; you can see it on the class page afterwards, along with any recordings your lecturer shares.
