1. **Marking**: objective questions mark themselves. Essays and practical criteria are marked in *Marking*; several examiners can mark the same script (averaged or reconciled).
2. **Moderation and approval**: the head of department or dean approves course results; the course lecturer cannot.
3. **Publishing**: the exams officer publishes approved results. Published results are locked; a later change needs a second officer and is recorded with the reason.
4. **Withheld results**: a candidate with an open incident has their result withheld until it is decided.

Students download statements of result and transcripts as signed PDFs, verifiable by anyone at the address printed on them.

## Question by question

From an exam's results, **Question by question** shows, for every question: how many students had it and answered it, how many got full marks, the average mark and the facility, how often each option was chosen (and for multiple true/false, how often each statement was judged right), and any reports. **Scores per question** downloads the old system's sheet: first, middle and last name, username, matric, Q1…Qn, total, out of, percentage, final score, grade, time taken and status.

Each student's **script** shows what they had, what they chose, the right answer and the mark. **Correct this answer** changes what the student chose (for example when the answer sheet shows a different option); with a reason, it waits for a second officer, then the script is marked again — even for approved results. Your institution can let approvers correct at once (Settings › Policy).

**Overview and scoreboard** is the live board: students, writing now, submitted, average, median, highest, lowest, the score bands, and every score out of any total (100, 70, 30…), with a download.
