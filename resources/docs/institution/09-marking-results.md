1. **Marking**: objective questions mark themselves. Essays and practical criteria are marked in *Marking*; several examiners can mark the same script (averaged or reconciled).
2. **Moderation and approval**: the head of department or dean approves course results; the course lecturer cannot.
3. **Publishing**: the exams officer publishes approved results. Published results are locked; a later change needs a second officer and is recorded with the reason.
4. **Withheld results**: a candidate with an open incident has their result withheld until it is decided.

Students download statements of result and transcripts as signed PDFs, verifiable by anyone at the address printed on them.
