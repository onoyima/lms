## Adding people

- **One by one**: Administration > People > Add.
- **From a spreadsheet**: Administration > People > Import, with the columns `name, email, identifier, type, roles` (download the **People** template). Existing e-mail addresses are updated, not duplicated. Up to 5,000 rows per file.
- **From the student information system**: connect it under Integrations; students, courses and registrations then arrive automatically.

New people receive a link to set their own password; nobody (including administrators) ever sees a password.

## Giving and removing access

- Roles are given on each person's row. You can only give roles whose permissions you hold yourself.
- **Temporary access** (Delegate): give a role until a date — it is removed automatically.
- **Access review** (Administration > Access review): every 90 days (set in Policy) someone confirms who still needs which role. The dashboard reminds you when it is due.

## Identity checks

Administration > Identity checks lists face enrolments waiting for a person to compare with the ID photo, and lets you upload ID photos in bulk. Face data is encrypted and can be erased on request.
