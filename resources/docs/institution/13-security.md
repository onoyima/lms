- **Two-factor sign-in** is required for the roles listed in Policy; single sign-on with your own SAML or OpenID provider is under Single sign-on.
- **Audit trail**: every change to questions, papers, marks, results, roles and settings is recorded in a chain that shows any later tampering.
- **Settings history**: Policy > History lists every saved version and what changed; *Roll back to this* restores one (and is itself a version).
- **Campus settings**: each campus can have its own exam-day contacts, candidate notice, exams-per-day limit and attendance rule.
- **Evidence** (snapshots, recordings, face data) is kept only as long as your evidence policy says, then deleted automatically, unless an incident or appeal is open.
- **E-mail delivery**: Operations > E-mail delivery shows sent, failed and bounced messages.
