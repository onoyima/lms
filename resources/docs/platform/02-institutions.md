## Applications

Institutions apply from the public website. Before approving, **check they are genuine**: call the phone number, confirm the e-mail domain belongs to the institution. Approving creates the institution, starts the trial (30 days by default) and e-mails the administrator a link to set a password. Declining records the reason.

## Adding an institution directly

Institutions > Add institution — for signed contracts. Give the short name (its web address), domains, plan, billing cycle, trial days and the first administrator.

## On each institution's page

- **Subscription**: change plan or cycle, set an agreed price, issue a renewal invoice.
- **Contract details**: contacts and domains.
- **Modules**: switch a module on as an add-on or off, on top of the plan. See *Modules, one by one* below.
- **Suspend / restore**: reasons are *unpaid*, *institution asked*, *misuse*, *other*. Paying lifts only an *unpaid* suspension.
- **Invoices and payments**: record a bank transfer (check the statement first), cancel an unpaid invoice.


## Modules, one by one

Every part of the platform is a separate module, switched per institution on its page (Modules). Switching one off hides its menus, dims any link to it, and refuses its pages and API; nothing else changes. Examples:

- An institution with **only Teaching (LMS)**: courses, materials, assignments, discussions and calendar work; quizzes that need the question bank are hidden.
- An institution with **only the exam board**, **only live classes**, or **only exams**: the same, each on its own.

The main modules are Question bank & exam delivery ("Exams"), Teaching (LMS), Live classes & meetings, Results & governance, Analytics, Browser proctoring, Live proctoring, CBT hall operations, Secure clients and the Test-series shop. Some smaller parts **come with** a main module and can still be switched off on their own:

| Part | Comes with |
|---|---|
| Exam board, Computer checks, Face identity, My results | Exams |
| Course reviews, polls & requests | Teaching (LMS) |
| Certificates, Requests & exceptions, AI tools, Text messages | Every institution |

Parts that come with a module do not count towards the plan's module allowance. Switching a part off (for example *My results*) leaves its main module working.
