The platform console is where the platform team runs the service. It is deliberately separate from the institutions:

- Platform administrators manage **institutions, applications, plans and prices, modules, invoices and payments, the team, system health and security**.
- They hold **no institution permissions**. Opening any institution page redirects back here; the console reads only counts (students, staff, sittings) for billing by size.
- Institution people can never open `/platform`.
- Every action here is written to the platform audit chain.

Two-factor sign-in is required for every platform administrator.
